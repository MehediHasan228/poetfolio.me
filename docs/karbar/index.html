What it does

Built on Laravel & Filament, extended for local compliance and workflows.

NBR VAT — Mushak

Mushak 6.3 tax invoices generated from posted invoices, 6.6 VDS certificates, 6.5 transfer challans and 9.1 returns, with fiscal-year serials.

BD Payroll

Salary structures, income-tax TDS with exemption & rebate, PF, gratuity, festival bonus, unpaid leave, payslip PDF and BEFTN bank advice.

Project Dashboard

Project health, burn-down, team workload, billable hours and upcoming deadlines with drill-down filters.

Accounting & Invoicing

Chart of accounts, journals, customer invoices, vendor bills, payments and financial reports in BDT.

Inventory, Sales & Purchase

Warehouses, stock, barcode operations, quotations, sales and purchase orders, manufacturing and maintenance.
